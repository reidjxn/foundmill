## Decisions
### The arrivals line is a count and nothing else
Founder's D1 (Nov 16): one private line with a count, nothing about who. No name, no link, no public count. Only the credited founder reads it; a second founder of the project reads their own count, a member of another project reads 0.
### The person page is not mounted for the app prompt yet
Pull requests #208 and #217 both edit `apps/web/app/u/[handle]/page.tsx`. One line (`<PublicAppPrompt />`) follows when they merge.
### D2's answer of Nov 16 is superseded (founder, Nov 17, 2026)
The first answer was to keep the people layer closed until five conditions held. The fact is that `LIVE_MODE` is 1 on the api in production, and the founder, told so, said "Yes, leave it at 1". The layer stays open for the accounts already let in; the same five conditions now gate opening sign-up (WP-00 S5). The Nov 16 entry is history and stays as written; this entry replaces its D2.
### Persona drawn at random is left out of the Today comparison
The Chief of Staff's persona key is random per project, so the open-versus-closed comparison blanks it, along with ids and dates.
