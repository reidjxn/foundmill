# DESIGN.md — design system art direction

The design system of Foundmill lives in `packages/dugout` (the package name is internal; the product never shows it). This document is the taste. `docs/BUILD.md` §5 is the contract (tokens, components, keyboard); this file says how it must *feel* and look. When the two seem to disagree, BUILD.md wins on facts and this file wins on aesthetics.

## 1. The feeling

**A broadcast control room at night, built for one founder: precise, lit and unmistakably 2027.**

The room is dark and deep; the instruments are lit. Panels are machined plates whose 1 px edge catches light at two corners. Numbers are tabular and quiet, with their currency and units dimmed so the figure reads first. Status glows softly in its own color. Department color lives inside a panel as light from its lit corner, never as a painted fill. The brand slab across the top is deep glass with a light edge, and it lights the canvas beneath it. Generating is a visible live process, never a modal wait. Agents are crew you talk to: they have faces, presence and a voice, and the founder can always answer with one press.

Three words: **Composed. Dense. Alive.**

It should feel like a financial terminal and the best management sim crossed with a modern instrument cluster, finished by a motion designer who respects the founder's attention. Dense but never cramped. Quiet but never dead. Every pixel earns its place, and nothing is ever clipped.

What it is not: a SaaS dashboard of cards floating on white; radii of 16 px or more; heavy drop shadows; glows on static panels or on text; pastel gradients; generic "AI" violet or sparkle; frosted glass everywhere; film grain, scanlines or decorative noise; emoji; uppercase buttons, tabs and titles (the tell of 2021). No light theme exists.

## 2. Non-negotiables

- Desktop only. Reference 1440 px, minimum 1280 px. Below that render `NeedsLargerWindow` and nothing else.
- Dark only. One theme.
- **Structure never moves.** Sidebar 232, top bar 56, processing strip 3, tab bar 40, inbox list 380, reading measure 640; the same panels in the same grid positions with the same content. A visual change restyles a region; it never resizes or moves it.
- Tokens only. No hex outside `tokens.css`. No arbitrary Tailwind values like `bg-[#123456]` or `p-[13px]`; add a token instead. Spacing from the 4/8 px scale; exact pixel sizes through px tokens (`h-control-md`, `h-field`, …).
- Lucide icons only. 16 px default (14 px inline in text, 20 px in empty states), stroke 1.75. The generate control's glyph is Lucide **Fan** (the mill).
- Fonts: Barlow Semi Condensed 600 (figures, titles, data labels), Inter 400/500/600 (everything you read or press), JetBrains Mono variable (mono 450, micro 500). Self-hosted through `@fontsource` packages. No font CDN.
- **WCAG AA is the floor.** Content text reaches 4.5 : 1 on every surface it sits on; `text-3` is tuned so it does on the canvas, the rail and surfaces 1–4 (a unit test checks it). `text-4` is for disabled controls only. No text below 11 px except kbd glyphs.
- Color is never the only signal: pills carry an icon and a word, ratings carry a number, deltas carry an arrow, presence carries words ("Live · typing", "Away"), a dashed edge always means "not measured".
- `prefers-reduced-motion` turns every animation into an instant state change with identical information: every loop stops on a legible frame, streams render whole.
- Effects are CSS (keyframes, `@property`, pseudo-elements), so the server-safe components (Chip, StatusPill, Panel, Skeleton, Kbd…; `clientBoundaries.test.ts`) stay server components.
- **Never clip.** Buttons, chips, pills and tabs never shrink below their label: rows wrap; fixed slots step the font down to a floor, then wrap to two lines, then truncate with a tooltip as the last resort; numbers never truncate. Tabs overflow into a "More" menu.
- Never use Football Manager, Sports Interactive or SEGA names, art, skins, icons or fonts. Reproduce the layout grammar only.

## 3. Surfaces and depth

Depth comes from light: a deep canvas, a short ladder of surfaces, alpha hairlines that sit on any surface, a gradient edge that catches light, and a soft elevation ramp. Everything raised is lit from above.

| Layer | Token (utility) | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas | `--canvas` (`bg-canvas`; alias `bg-base`) | #08090C | The page. A faint light in the brand's aura color (11 %) sits under the top bar, as if the slab lights the room (`canvas-lit` on the shell; Foundmill steel on frames without a business). No scanlines, no noise. |
| Rail | `--rail` (`bg-rail`) | #0B0D11 | Sidebar, list columns |
| Surface 1 | `--surface-1` (`bg-surface-1`; alias `bg-panel`) | #0F1217 | Panel body, with a 1.8 % white top light over its first 120 px |
| Surface 2 | `--surface-2` (`bg-surface-2`; alias `bg-panel-alt`) | #151920 | Raised: secondary buttons, quick replies, hover rows, bubbles |
| Surface 3 | `--surface-3` (`bg-surface-3`) | #1C212A | Active segment, menus, hover cards |
| Surface 4 | `--surface-4` (`bg-surface-4`) | #252B36 | Pressed |
| Ink | `--ink` (`bg-ink`) | #05070A | Terminals, diffs, code, the generation log |

**Lines** are white at low alpha, so one set works on every layer: `line-faint` 4.5 % (row dividers), `line` 7.5 % (hairlines, the panel header rule; alias `border-subtle`), `line-strong` 13 % (control borders), `edge-hi` 24 % (light caught at a corner). Dividers inside panels fade out towards their end (`rule-fade`); they are never hard full-width rules.

**The gradient edge.** A panel's 1 px border is a 145° gradient: `edge-hi` at the top left, falling to 7 % by 16 %, 3.5 % through the middle, rising to 15 % at the bottom right. `surface-panel` paints it in the border box behind a transparent border, so a caller's border color (a gold or red edge) still replaces it. `edge` draws the same ring on any borderless element; `edge-tint` puts a department or brand color (`tone-*`) into the lit corner.

**Elevation** (soft, layered, low alpha, always with an inner top highlight):

| Token | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `elev-0` | Flush tiles |
| `elev-1` | Panels |
| `elev-2` | Hover cards, menus, popovers, sticky bars (the DecisionBar) |
| `elev-3` | Dialogs, the command palette |
| `ring` | Focus: 1 px accent at 65 % plus a 4 px halo at 16 % |

**Glows** are colored light from a source, and only from a source: status dots and badges, the Today line, the active sidebar edge, the generate control, Continue, running processing-strip segments, presence rings. `shadow-glow-good|warn|bad|info|accent|gold|kit|tone`. Never on a static panel, never on text.

- **Panel** (`surface-panel`): surface 1, 10 px radius, the gradient edge, the top light, `elev-1`. Department panels add `glow-dept` with `tone-dept-N`: a radial of the department color at 13 % from the top-left corner (90 % × 140 px) and, with `edge-tint`, a tinted lit corner. Used on department cards, the note's metric strip, the agent thread and concept cards. Brand-lit panels (`glow-brand`): the Journey and the Ideation brief.
- **Panel header**: 40 px on the panel's own surface — **no striped band**. Title in the title style (Inter 600 13, sentence case), meta in mono `text-3` ("Aug 6 – Nov 8, 2026", "4 of 5 done"), then on the right an Estimate or Forecast chip and a ghost `…` menu. A 1 px rule fades out to the right under it.
- **Raised controls** (`surface-raised`): surface 2 with a 5.5 %→2 % white sheen, a `line-strong` ring and a rim. **Wells** (`surface-well`): black at 30 % over the panel with a `line-strong` ring and an inset shadow: every input, select and the segmented track.
- **Overlays** (`surface-popover`: menus, popovers, hover cards, select lists, toasts): glass — surface 3 at 86 % over a 16 px blur — a `line-strong` hairline whose top edge catches a little more light, 14 px radius, `elev-2`; they scale from .98 at their trigger's origin as they fade in (`overlay-motion`, 200 ms in, 140 ms out). Tooltips and chart tips (`surface-overlay`) are the same glass on a 10 px radius: they are labels, not surfaces. **Dialogs and the command palette** (`surface-dialog`): the same glass, 14 px radius, `elev-3`, scaling from their center; the title line keeps clear of the close button and the footer is a darker strip under a hairline. Dialog backdrop: the canvas at 66 % with a 6 px blur. Menu items are 32 px on a 6 px radius with an 8 % highlight (12 % red for danger); a trigger's chevron turns over while its menu or list is open.
- **Toasts**: a stack at the bottom left of the content column, beside the sidebar at its real width, expanded or collapsed (over the Inbox list's foot, never over the DecisionBar, Continue or a dialog's footer), 340 px wide. Pages without a sidebar (first run, sign-in) put it bottom right, never on the first column of cards (`useToastPlacement` / `<ToastPlacement>`). Each toast is a tone tile (a 28 px square of status light holding the icon), a title, an optional line and action, and a 2 px timer along its foot that pauses with the toast. The newest is in front; older ones peek 10 px behind it, smaller, and fan out into a list on hover or focus. Swipe left dismisses.
- **Glass on the brand slab** (`glass`): black at 30 % over a 14 px blur, an 11 % ring and a 10 % top highlight. Only on the top bar.
- **Terminals and diffs** sit on `ink` so code reads as code.
- **Table rows**: `line-faint` dividers; hover at 3.5 % white; selected rows get a 3 px `kit-lift` left edge with its glow.
- **Scrollbars**: 6 px, thumb `line-strong` (`text-4` on hover), track transparent.
- **Focus**: keyboard only. Every focusable element shows an accent outline 2 px out that follows its radius; fields and cards use the `ring` token instead (`focus-ring`, `focus-within-ring`).

## 4. Typography

**Case rules.** Sentence case for every action, tab, sidebar item, panel title, form label, chip and pill. Caps appear only on data labels, set by CSS (`t-data`), never typed. Barlow is for figures and titles; Inter for everything you read or press; mono for timestamps, tokens, ids, provenance and logs.

| Style (utility) | Spec | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| display-hero (`t-display-hero`) | Barlow Semi Condensed 600, 80/80 | The signed-out landing page's three headlines only |
| figure-hero (`t-figure-hero`) | Barlow 600, 54/56, tabular | The one hero figure per screen (the Journey headline) |
| display-xl (`t-display-xl`) | Barlow 600, 40/44 | Win moment, landing section titles |
| display-lg (`t-display-lg`) | Barlow 600, 30/32, tabular | Tile figures, status strip figures |
| display-md (`t-display-md`) | Barlow 600, 20/24 (top-bar title 21) | Screen titles, profile names, note subjects at 25/30 |
| display-sm (`t-display-sm`) | Barlow 600, 16/20 | Business name, card names (19/22 on concept cards) |
| title (`t-title`) | Inter 600, 13/18, −0.003em | Panel titles |
| action (`t-action`; alias `t-tab`) | Inter 500, 13/16 | Buttons, tabs, sidebar items, quick replies, segmented options |
| action-sm (`t-action-sm`; alias `t-tab-sm`) | Inter 500, 12.5/16 | Small buttons |
| label (`t-label`) | Inter 500, 12/16, `text-2` | Form labels, table headers, chips |
| **data** (`t-data`) | Barlow 600, 10.5/14, caps, +0.09em, `text-3` | The only caps: tile captions, column heads of data grids, "Last 7 days", "Your call", Estimate and Forecast |
| body (`t-body`) / strong (`t-body-strong`) | Inter 400 / 500, 13/19 | Everything else; subjects and names in rows |
| reading (`t-reading`) | Inter 400, 15/24, −0.006em, `text-wrap: pretty`, the reading color #DFE4EA | Inbox note body only (a 640 px measure) |
| chat (`t-chat`) | Inter 400, 13.5/20 | Conversation bubbles |
| small (`t-small`) | Inter 400, 12/16 | Captions, metadata |
| mono (`t-mono`) | JetBrains Mono 450, 12/18, tabular | Timestamps, tokens, provenance, ids, counts in headers, logs, diffs |
| micro (`t-micro`) | JetBrains Mono 500, 11/12, +0.02em | Axis ticks, kbd |

**Figures.** Tabular figures go on numbers only — figures, tables, counters, meters, timestamps (`tabular`, and built into the display and mono styles) — never on body text, so prose punctuation is never spaced. Numbers right-align in tables. Figure micro-typography: the currency symbol is smaller, dimmed and raised (`t-currency`: 66 %, `text-3`), decimals are dimmed (`t-decimals`: 66 %, `text-2`), units are mono (`t-unit`: 62 %, `text-3`: "/day", "of 12"). £72K reads as a dim £, a bright 72, a dim K. Big numbers never animate digit by digit; they settle once. Titles use `text-wrap: balance`.

## 5. Color

- **Text** is an AA ramp: `text-1` #EDF0F4 content (`text-primary`); `text-2` #A9B3C1 secondary text and labels (`text-secondary`); `text-3` #8A94A3 captions, axes, units, metadata, provenance, read receipts, counters and token meters (`text-tertiary`); `text-4` #5A6370 **disabled only**, never meaningful content (`text-disabled`). Placeholders are `text-3`. The note body uses the reading color #DFE4EA (`text-prose`).
- **Accent** #7CC4FF (`text-accent`): focus, selection, links outside prose. It is not an entity color.
- **Entity links** (founder, 2026-10-09): **no icon, the text's own color, always underlined** — 1 px at 40 % of the text color, offset 0.24em, inline so long names wrap (`entity-link`). Hover or keyboard focus brings the underline to full strength and opens the hover card after 300 ms. An unverified mention uses a dotted underline (`entity-link-dotted`). Plain in-text links (not entities) follow the same treatment.
- **Status**: `status-good` #3DD68C, `status-warn` #F5B841, `status-bad` #F2545B, `status-info` #5B8CFF. Pills are icon + word in the status color on a 12 % fill with a 30 % ring, 22 px tall, fully round; any pulse is in the pill's own tone, never the brand. Never a bare colored dot without a word nearby.
- **Rating ramp (1–20)**: `rating-1` red for 1–5, `rating-2` amber for 6–10, `rating-3` light green #9ADF7E for 11–15, `rating-4` bold green for 16–20 (also set in 600). Skill bars are 20 discrete cells; lit cells take the ramp color with a top light and a faint glow; empty cells are white at 5 %. In the skill input the 20 cells *are* the slider's track.
- **Brand colors** (the business's primary and secondary, `--kit-primary` / `--kit-secondary`) are its identity. From the primary the shell derives a ramp in OKLCH (`kitRamp`, written by `kitStyle`): `kit-lift` (the brand lifted towards `text-1`, always ≥ 7 : 1 on the canvas: lines, edges, the Today line, carets, the active sidebar icon), `kit-hi` (active edges, lit corners), `kit-lo` and `kit-deep` (the gradient's low stops, wells), `kit-aura` (light: halos and the canvas light). Any brand color therefore lights correctly; frames without a business use Foundmill steel (#3570CC, lift #7CC4FF). The brand is used with restraint, only here: the top-bar slab and the logo tile, primary actions (Continue at rest, Approve, Save and continue) in a brand gradient, the generate control's core tile, the active sidebar item (tinted plate, lit 3 px edge), the revenue line and Today on the Journey, the Channel Board hub, the Win moment, the initials mark that stands in for a missing logo, the selected-row edge, a landed card's edge flash. `kit-on-primary` is computed (white or near-black) so text on the brand passes APCA Lc ≥ 75 and WCAG 4.5 : 1; a brand color where neither can is flagged when it is chosen.
- **No AI purple.** A drafting card's live edge, caret and log name use the drafting agent's own department color or `kit-lift`; violet is department 5's color and nothing else.
- **Department colors** `--dept-1` … `--dept-8`: #5B8CFF blue, #3DD68C green, #F5B841 amber, #F2545B red, #B98CFF violet, #4FD1C5 teal, #FF8A5B orange, #E879C8 pink. Used for Ribbon bands, department dots (with their glow), processing-strip segments, sidebar badges, avatar palettes, the light inside department panels, and the 4 % tint of an agent's chat bubbles (its voice color). Set any of them as the local tone with `tone-dept-N`.
- **Gold** `--gold` #E8C56B (gradient to #B8923A) is reserved for Customer Updates, milestones, customers and stars.

## 6. Spacing and sizes

8 px grid, 4 px half-steps for inline tightness. Panel padding 12 px. Content gutters 16 px. Sidebar 232 px (64 px collapsed). Top bar 56 px. Processing strip 3 px. Tab bar 40 px. Right rail 320 px. Inbox list 380 px. Reading measure 640 px. Panel header 40 px. Rows 28 px compact, 34 px comfortable.

The app's root font size is 13 px, so Tailwind's numeric spacing runs at 13/16 of its nominal pixels (`h-8` is 26 px). Anything that must match a pixel spec uses a px token:

| Token | Height | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `control-xs` (`h-control-xs`; `h-chip`) | 22 | Chips, pills, tags |
| `control-sm` | 28 | Small buttons, the segmented track |
| `control-md` | 32 | Buttons, sidebar rows, quick replies |
| `control-lg` | 40 | Large primary, decision buttons (38 inside the DecisionBar) |
| `field` / `field-lg` | 36 / 46 | Text fields / MoneyInput and selects in forms |
| `control-xl` | 48 | The generate control (small variant 28 with a 20 px core) |
| `badge` | 18 | Badges, Estimate and Forecast chips, kbd |

Radii: `r-xs` 3 (cells, Estimate chips, kbd), `r-sm` 6 (controls, chips, sidebar items; `rounded-panel` is this value), `r-md` 10 (panels, tiles, cards, overlays), `r-lg` 14 (dialogs, chat bubbles with 5 at the tail; the generate control uses 12), `r-pill` (status pills, badges, presence, avatars). Nothing reaches 16. Avatars: 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, 36, 48, 64 (the conversation's presence portrait 64–68).

## 7. Motion

Motion carries meaning or it does not exist. Three curves: `ease-out` cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) for hovers, overlays and reveals; `ease-spring` cubic-bezier(.34,1.45,.64,1) for press release, the slider thumb and chip pops; `ease-inout` cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1) for sliding indicators. Linear for progress, orbit, shimmer and streaming.

| Duration | ms | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| press | 90 | Press feedback: 1 px down and .985 (`pressable`), springing back on release |
| hover | 140 | Hover |
| overlay | 200 | Overlays: scale .98 → 1 from their origin, plus fade |
| slide | 260 | Tab underline, segmented plate (`slide-indicator`, `slide-plate`) |
| view | 320 | Route and tab cross-fades; a landed card rising in |
| pulse | 600 | One-shot ring (Continue, a live event) |
| draw | 1200 | Journey line draw; a landed card's edge settling |
| shimmer | 1600 | Skeleton band, processing heads |
| breathe | 2400 | Presence ring, the generate control's orbit |

Cadences: typing dots loop every 1200 ms, 160 ms apart (`typing-dots`); streaming text runs at 22–34 ms a character with a 120 ms pause at commas; the caret blinks on 1060 ms steps, only while a stream is open (`stream-caret`); skeleton bars start 60 ms apart and quick replies rise 40 ms apart (`stagger-N`). Scripts read the same values from `lib/motion.ts`.

- **Continue**: at rest the brand gradient. When a cycle finishes and new items arrive, one 600 ms ring pulse, never a loop. Blocked is a solid red gradient with ink text "Respond · N", an alert icon and a red halo, with one ring pulse when it turns blocked. Processing is a dark slab with an orbiting edge and the label "Pause". A Customer Update is gold. Vacation is neutral with "Come back".
- **Generate** (the signature control; there is one per surface and it is the only light slab in the product): at rest a 48 px light slab with the brand core tile holding the Fan glyph and a brand halo; on hover a sheen crosses once (700 ms). Running: a dark slab, a light arc orbiting its border (one turn per 2400 ms), the fan turning (1400 ms a turn), a bottom rail tracking real progress, a mono count ("2 of 12") and **Stop** beside it.
- **Processing strip**: a running segment carries a bright head traveling in 1600 ms with an 8 px glow in its department color; paused segments are dim; idle capacity is a 5 % track.
- **Journey Ribbon**: the line draws left to right over 1.2 s on first load. Live events ripple into the Today line with a ring. The Today line glows in `kit-lift`.
- **Hover cards**: 300 ms delay, then scale from the link and fade over 200 ms.
- **Tabs and segmented controls**: the indicator slides to the new option in 260 ms; it never jumps.
- **Sidebar collapse**: 200 ms width, labels fade in 140 ms after.
- **Presence**: a live agent's ring breathes outwards (scale 1 → 1.35, fading, 2400 ms) (`presence-breathe`).
- **Landed cards**: rise 8 px and fade in (320 ms), flash a `kit-lift` edge that settles over 1200 ms, and carry a "Just in" tag (`landed`).
- **Win moment**: a 1.2 s full-bleed brand wipe with a PixiJS particle burst in the brand's secondary color and gold; sound off by default. Reduced motion shows a static gold card with the same facts.
- **Tables**: no row animation. Selection and sort are instant.
- **Skeletons**: surface-2 bars with a 100° light band (1600 ms), staggered; never spinners inside panels. A skeleton names its queue ("Queued for Teodor").
- **Landing page only** (§9a): a block that starts below the fold rises 24 px and fades in once as it enters the window (`--dur-reveal` 720 ms, `[data-reveal]`); the activity ticker drifts left once every 80 s (`animate-marquee`, paused on hover); an auto-advancing tab shows its timer as a 1 px line filling left to right (`animate-progress`).
- **Reduced motion**: every loop above stops on a legible frame (the orbit's arc rests, the caret is solid, the shimmer is a flat bar, the presence ring is the static status ring), streams render whole, nothing advances by itself.

## 8. Component anatomy (the pieces that define the look)

- **AppShell**: sidebar left, the top bar, the processing strip under it, the tab bar, the content grid, an optional right rail. Nothing floats. The shell paints the lit canvas (`canvas-lit`) in the business's own aura.
- **Sidebar**: 232 px on the rail, a hairline right edge that catches light at the top. Items 32 px, 10 px in from the edges, 6 px radius: a 16 px icon in `text-3` and an action-style label in `text-2`. Hover adds a 3.5 % plate. Active: a brand plate (primary at 30 % fading to 7 % left to right) with a 16 % `kit-lift` ring, the icon in `kit-lift`, and a **3 px lit edge** at the rail's edge with its glow. Badges are 18 px pills in the department color with an ink number and a glow. Groups are separated by hairlines that fade out. Foot: Settings, Anthropic key, then the Foundmill wordmark (§8a). Collapsed keeps icons and badges and draws no brand at all.
- **Top bar** (`KitBar`): 56 px, a **glass slab** in the brand color. Layers from the top: a 10 % white gloss, a brand gradient from 92 % to 38 % alpha left to right over a near-black, a fixed 104° specular streak (`specular`); a 1 px light top edge, a `kit-lift` line under the slab, and a brand glow cast onto the canvas. Text is `kit-on-primary`. Left: the logo tile 32 px (7 px radius, inner ring), business name (display-sm), back/forward. The logo and name are the business switcher: a chevron after the name, the tooltip “Switch business”; it opens a glass popover headed “Switch business” with the founder's businesses (24 px logos, the current one first and checked, then A to Z, role and Demo chips), a filter field from six businesses, then Create a new business, Manage businesses, Home and Your profile. Center: screen title (display-md, 21) and universal search. Right: date + runway chip, business-mode chip, **Continue**, **Stop all agents**. Without a runway figure the chip says what Home's runway tile says, from the one shared wording ("No limit" when cash-flow positive, "Not known yet" without a bank or with too little history), the reason as its tooltip; never a dash. Chips and search are `glass`, 34 px, 8 px radius; the search placeholder is short enough never to be cut ("Search people, companies, agents"). The runway's Estimate tag is the dashed chip. Every control stays whole at 1280 and 1440: the search gives way first (its placeholder shortens, then only the icon), then the chips compact one step at a time (`KitBarFit`: dates drop the year, the vacation caption shortens to "Until Oct 21" and then to its icon, LIVE and the mode show their icons, the date leaves the runway chip) while the screen title keeps its room.
- **TabBar**: 40 px, transparent over the lit canvas with a hairline under it. Tabs in the action style, `text-3` → `text-1`, sentence case. The active tab has a 2 px `text-1` bar with a soft white glow that **slides** between tabs (260 ms). Counts sit in a pill; Respond's count is red with ink text. Tabs never shrink below their label; overflow goes into a "More" menu.
- **Panel**: see §3. Empty state: a 20 px Lucide icon on a soft disc of department light (`icon-halo`, `tone`), one line of body text, one small button or an action slot (a small generate control, "Draft with Juno", when the team can fill the panel). Error state: `status-bad` pill + one line, technical detail behind a disclosure on `ink`. Spinners only inside buttons and inline controls.
- **Buttons**: sentence case, Inter 500, the icon before the label, 6 px radius; press 1 px down and .985, release springs back. **Primary**: the brand gradient (20 % lighter at the top), white or computed text, an inner top highlight and a brand glow below. **Secondary** (default): `surface-raised`. **Ghost**: `text-2`; hover adds a 5 % plate and `text-1`. **Danger**: a bright red gradient with near-black text (the blocked Continue). **Gold**: Customer Updates and milestones only. **Active toggle**: a tinted ring and soft glow in the context color. A button can carry an inline caps tag ("Approve · Recommended").
- **Generate**: see §7. Per card, a small generate pill labeled for founders ("More like this"), never git jargon. In code: `Button variant="generate"` (sizes sm 28 / md / lg / xl 48; `running`, real `progress` 0–1, a `count` chip, a `kbd` hint at rest) and `GenerateControl`, which puts **Stop** beside it while it runs. Two-line options are `Button description="…"` (with `tag="Recommended"`); an active toggle is `Button active tone="warn"`.
- **FitText** (the never-clip rule in code): a label in a fixed slot steps its font down in 0.5 px to a floor (11 px for body text, 75 % for small display, 60 % for figures), then wraps to two lines when asked (`lines={2}`), then truncates with a tooltip; ids and emails truncate in the middle; numbers never truncate (`truncate={false}` with a compact `fallback`). One shared ResizeObserver measures every instance; a hidden ghost copy keeps the box's width, so fitting never moves the layout. `Button fit`, `Chip fit` and `StatusPill fit` use it; by default chips and pills wrap instead.
- **Quick replies**: always 2–4 under the last agent message whenever the founder is expected to respond, 32 px `surface-raised` buttons with a leading kbd digit (1–4 send directly; Alt+1–4 in the Inbox). The likeliest reply is brand-tinted; a ghost "Write" opens the composer, so typing is optional, never required. They rise in 40 ms apart.
- **Inputs**: the wrapper is the field (`surface-well`, 36 px, 6–8 px radius); prefix, value and suffix sit in normal flow, so they cannot overlap. Focus lights the whole field (`focus-within-ring`) with an accent caret. **MoneyInput**: symbol in Barlow 18 `text-3`, the value in Barlow 600 22 grouped live (60,000) and tabular, the ISO code as its own segment behind a hairline in mono `text-1`. The currency select is its own 46 px field (code chip, name, chevron). Help line underneath: an info icon, the sentence, and an Estimate chip.

## 10. Writing in the interface

Plain, specific, short. Sentence case everywhere: actions, tabs, titles, labels, chips and pills. Caps only on data labels, set by CSS (`t-data`), never typed. No exclamation marks. No "Oops". No emoji. Numbers carry units. Non-real numbers carry "Forecast" or "Estimate", spelled in full, in a dashed chip next to them (or as the word in `text-3` after the number inside reading prose). Empty states say what will appear and how to make it happen. Agents speak in the first person, briefly and specifically, and a typing line names what the agent is looking at.

**US English and US dates** (founder, 2026-10-10). Every word a person reads is American English: color, organization, judgment, center, behavior, favorite, labeled, canceled, catalog, program, gray, while, among, learned. The founder's away mode is **Vacation** ("On vacation until Oct 21"); the Finance tab is **Payroll**. Dates read "Oct 7, 2026", "Oct 7" when the year is implied, "Wed, Oct 7" with the weekday, "Oct 2026" for a month, and "Oct 7 – 21" for a range; times are 12-hour ("3:45 PM"); relative times stay as they are ("2 days ago"). Numbers are grouped the en-US way. Money keeps the business's own currency and its narrow symbol, formatted en-US (a GBP business still reads "£1,240.00"), and a business's real-world facts keep their form (GBP, VAT, "Ltd"). Design-system dates come from `DATE_FORMATS` in `packages/dugout/src/lib/format.ts`; apps/web's own helpers (`apps/web/lib/format.ts`) print in UTC so the server's HTML and the browser's hydration agree, and a time they print says "UTC". The top bar's date and the vacation caption are the founder's own day, so they appear once the browser knows it (`apps/web/lib/today.ts`): on a US evening UTC is already tomorrow. A stored key shown in words (`prioritisation`, `categoriser`, `holiday.read_authority`) goes through `usSpelling` and reads US English ("Prioritization", "Categorizer", "Vacation read authority"); a table cell never shows a raw key as its tooltip. `node scripts/us-english.mjs` (CI) fails on a British spelling or a day-first date in user-facing text.
