# Founder notices and the former founder's cancel (security review P2-3)

Branch `claude/founder-notices`, on `claude/waitlist-invitations` (migrations 0091–0096 there, after main's 0090). One migration here, **0097** (written as 0094, renumbered on Nov 13, 2026 when #120 took 0091–0096). Reworked in place after the second and third security reviews (same day): it has not been merged anywhere.

## The problem

Someone with founder standing (an owner or a cofounder) could lower another founder's role, including the one who created the project, then remove them, then ask for the project's deletion, and nobody else was told. The deletion's warning went only to the Inbox of the people still in the project, so a removed founder never saw it and could not cancel.

## What changed

1. **Every founder is emailed about these changes**, and so is the person it happened to, even once they are out:
   - a founder's role goes below a founder's (owner or cofounder to anything lower);
   - a founder is removed (a founder is always stepped down first, so a removal counts as a founder's when someone else lowered their founder's role in the last 30 days; the audit entry names it as `formerRole`);
   - deletion is asked for: every founder, **everyone someone else ever demoted or removed from the project** who is not a founder now, and a **receipt to the person who asked**;
   - deletion is canceled: the same people, never the one who canceled.

   The api sends ids only (`POST /v1/notify/email`, kind `founder_notice`, the audit entry's id); the gateway's one sender reads the entry, the people and the project again, and sends through `platform_notify`. The Inbox gets a Chief of Staff note for a lowered role or a removal (the deletion request and the cancel already had theirs; a cancel by a former founder now says who).
2. **A former founder for the request (someone else demoted or removed them within 30 days before it, or after it) can cancel it until its scheduled day**: from the one-click link in their email (`/a/<token>`, a POST to confirm) or from a card on their home (Today, Home and /new). It cancels that request only, restores nothing else (they stay out; rejoining is a normal invitation), writes their own audit entry (`formerFounder: true`, `requestId`, `via`), and every founder is told.
3. **The purge**: no change. The cancel right lasts until the request's own scheduled day, the day the sweep purges on, so nothing has to wait for it.
4. **Events**: one new account-scoped event, `founder_notice.sent`, on the reader's own account events (the reader may no longer belong to the project). `fm_account_event_types()` lists it (0097), the catalog declares it, a fixture and a stream-wall test cover it.
5. **Limits** (second and third reviews): founder notices have their own enforced daily ceiling (`pn:safety:day`, `PLATFORM_SAFETY_DAILY_CEILING`, default 5,000), no longer counted in `pn:all:day`, and a cap of 500 a day per project under it; one person hears about one project at most 10 times an hour and 30 a day, past which one "More changes in {project}" email stands for the rest of the window and the rest are recorded as `folded`. **Essential** notices (final review: one that carries the cancel; the removal's email to the person removed; a deletion request's email to a founder now, to whoever asked and to a former founder for that request) skip those ceilings and the per-project fold and have bounds of their own: 10 a day per person across every project, then one folded email for the day that carries the latest deletion that person may still cancel; and a circuit breaker over all of them (`PLATFORM_SAFETY_RESERVED_DAILY_CEILING`, default 20,000; a warning at half, an error at the ceiling; past it, the same per-person folded email). A deletion request (and its cancel) emails at most the 50 people someone else most recently took out of the project. In the api, one person may lower a founder's role, or ask for a deletion, 5 times a day in a project, and 10 times a day in all across every project (plain 429 refusals).

## Judgement calls

- **Classed as account-safety mail, apart from both existing classes.** There was no transactional or security class before: `EMAIL_NOTIFICATION_KINDS` are the person's own switchable kinds (with an off link, the pause, and three a day for most), `ADDRESS_EMAIL_KINDS` is the invitation (with "Don't send me invitations"). Following how security mail is classed elsewhere, a new `ACCOUNT_SAFETY_EMAIL_KINDS = ["founder_notice"]`: no switch in Settings, the pause does not hold it, none of the other kinds' counts, not the projects' room (`busy`), not "Don't send me invitations". Its own limits are below.
- **Its own ceiling, not the shared one** (P1-1). The first version counted toward `pn:all:day` without ever being refused, so two accounts could fill everyone's ceiling by flipping roles. Now `pn:safety:day` is separate and enforced (`safety_ceiling`), and `pn:all:day` is untouched by founder notices. A store that is not shared right now (Redis down) still counts on that replica rather than refusing: an outage should not silence an account-safety notice; the per-replica counts still hold the flood down.
- **Fold rather than drop, but never fold what matters** (P1-1, third review B). Past 10 an hour or 30 a day for one person and project, the first ordinary notice of the window becomes one "More changes" email, and every later ordinary one in that window is recorded as skipped with `folded` on its `founder_notifications` row, so nothing is dropped silently. An essential notice is never folded into the per-project window: an attacker who spends a person's window on harmless changes can no longer hide that person's removal, or a deletion request and its cancel, behind it. (Essential notices have their own, separate per-person day: below.) The marker for the window's "More changes" email is released when its send fails or another call recorded the notice first. The review's better form, a summary at the end of the window describing the state then (with any waiting deletion and its cancel), needs a scheduled pass to send it later; that is a larger change and is left out (below).
- **A reserved path past the ceilings** (third review A). With per-project actor limits only, about ten accounts across seventeen projects could fill the platform's 5,000 a day and silence every later founder notice. Now the actor's founder changes are also limited across every project (10 a day in all), each project has its own cap (500 a day) under the platform's, and an essential notice skips both on a reserved, counted path (`pn:safety:reserved:day`), so the notices a person needs most cannot be crowded out. **Correction (final review):** these notes said that what reaches the reserved path is "bounded by the actors' limits". It was not: each deletion request emailed everyone ever taken out of the project as essential mail, a list that cheap extra accounts can grow through invitations, so N colluding accounts could send about 10·N² essential emails a day, with no ceiling at all. The real bounds now are in the next entry.
- **The bounds on essential mail** (final review, P1 and P2). Essential is now exactly: an email that carries a cancel link; the removal's email to the person removed; and a deletion request's email to a founder now, to whoever asked (the receipt) and to a former founder for that request (in the 30-day window, link or not). The emails about a deletion and its cancel to someone taken out longer ago, and every cancel's email, are ordinary. The bounds, all of them enforced in the gateway:
  - **per person**: at most 10 essential emails a day across every project (`pn:safety:essential:<user>:day`), then one folded email for the day ("More changes to who runs your projects"), and every further essential notice that day is recorded as `folded`. So nobody gets more than 11 essential emails a day, however many accounts act;
  - **per request**: the founders now, the one who asked, and at most the 50 people someone else most recently took out of the project (`formerPerRequest`, applied in the api's fan-out and checked again in the gateway);
  - **platform-wide**: a circuit breaker at `PLATFORM_SAFETY_RESERVED_DAILY_CEILING` (default 20,000) a day, a warning logged at half and an error at the ceiling; past it, every essential notice takes the per-person folded path (each person's one folded email for the day, the rest recorded as `folded_reserved_ceiling`), never dropped silently. So the day's essential mail is at most the ceiling plus one email per person who has not yet had their folded one, and at most 11 per person;
  - the actor limits (5 a day per project, 10 a day in all) still hold, and still bound how many changes there are; they no longer have to bound the fan-out.
- **The folded email always carries the latest cancel the person may use.** When a person's essential share is used up, or the breaker has tripped, the day's one folded email looks for the latest waiting deletion that person is a former founder for, in any of their projects (`latestCancelFor`, as their home's card reads it). If the notice in hand already carries that request's link it is used; otherwise the gateway mints a new single-use link for it (`founder_cancel_links`, the same rules as the api's: one row per email, deleted again when the email is not sent, kept when the send threw, since it may have gone out), and the notice's own unused link is deleted. Later essential notices that day are folded; the home card still shows every deletion the person can cancel, and the folded email says so.
- **The deletion request is essential for the founders and the one who asked** (P2). Before, a full `pn:safety:day` could drop a deletion request's email to the founders still in the project and the requester's receipt. Now both are essential, under the per-person share above.
- **Zero is not "unset"** (P3). `PLATFORM_SAFETY_DAILY_CEILING` and `PLATFORM_SAFETY_RESERVED_DAILY_CEILING` are refused at boot when 0 or empty (an empty value reads as 0), with a message naming the variable and the default; in the gateway's config, a value that is set is used as it is, 0 included (it was treated as unset before).
- **A removal is announced once per demotion** (P3). `recentlyLowered` returned the same demotion for 30 days, so re-inviting someone and removing them again sent a fresh essential "removed" email each time. Now a removal counts as a founder's removal only while no `team.humans.remove` of that person has been recorded since the demotion.
- **A taken founder change is given back when its transaction fails** (P3). `takeFounderChange` returns its refund, and `team.humans.role` and `business.requestDeletion` run their transactions through `refundingOnFailure`, so a change that did not happen (a validation error after the take, a failed write) costs the person nothing.
- **The notices of one change are asked for 5 at a time** (P3, `NOTICE_CONCURRENCY`), not all at once.
- **SEC-8, written down, not changed here.** If the Supabase Data API ever exposed the `public` schema, `authenticated` could change `memberships` directly through RLS, with none of the api's limits (no actor limit, no notices: those live in the api). The 0097 trigger would still record every departure in `founder_departures`, so the cancel right and the deletion's emails would still reach the person, but nobody would be told at the time. One more reason the Data API must stay off (docs/SECURITY_REVIEW.md SEC-8; docs/DEPLOY.md records it off since Oct 10, 2026).
- **Limits on the actor in the api** (P1-1): lowering a founder's role and asking for a deletion, 5 a day per person and project, refused in plain words ("A deletion can be asked for 5 times a day by one person in a project, and that is used up for now. Try again in N hours."). A cancel is never limited: refusing it could let a deletion through, and each cancel needs a waiting request, which is limited.
- **Correction to the first version's notes**: they said each deletion request "needs a fresh sign-in". That was wrong: a request needs a sign-in within the last 10 minutes (`REAUTH_WINDOW_MS`), so one sign-in allows many requests. The 5-a-day limit above is what bounds them now.
- **Counted from the request, not the departure** (P2-1). The right to cancel belongs to someone whose standing someone else took away within 30 days before the request, or at any time after it, and who does not have it back, until that request's scheduled day. The first version's check that capped a token at 7 days after the departure is gone with the columns it sat on. A removal is announced as a founder's removal when the demotion was in the last 30 days.
- **Correction**: the second version of these notes said that "a founder cannot wait out a window before asking". That was wrong: someone who demotes or removes a founder and waits 31 days before asking for the deletion still keeps that founder from canceling it. What changes now (third review) is that they can no longer keep the removed founder from hearing about it: everyone someone else ever took out of the project gets every deletion request's email and its cancel's (without a link past the 30 days: "If you want it kept, ask one of the founders to cancel it."). The 30-day limit on the cancel right itself is kept as the review asked.
- **A receipt to whoever asks for a deletion** (P2-2), whatever the team's size: "You asked to delete {project} on {when}. It will be deleted on {day}. If this wasn't you, cancel in Settings › Danger zone and secure your account." It names nobody else and nothing about who else was told. No self-notice for a role change, a removal or a cancel.
- **Only a hard suppression stops it**: a bounce or a spam report (`HARD_SUPPRESSION_REASONS`, the words `suppressionReasonOf` writes, now shared constants), platform-wide or for that project. An unsubscribe from a project's mail or a "stop" reply does not. SendGrid's own bounce and spam lists still apply underneath. A member can no longer plant, change or delete a hard entry (P3-6): `suppression_list_write` now excludes those reasons for client roles (the service role, which writes them from delivery events, bypasses row security); a test checks the policy names the same reasons as the code. The flip side: a member can no longer remove a project's bounce entry from the app; only the service can.
- **SendGrid's global unsubscribe list** (P3-7): verified against SendGrid's v3 Mail Send reference ("bypass_unsubscribe_management: Allows you to bypass the global unsubscribe list … Bounce and spam report lists will still be checked … cannot be combined with the bypass_list_management filter"). Account-safety mail sets it (the SDK writes `mail_settings.bypass_unsubscribe_management`), and no longer sets `bypass_list_management`. Group unsubscribes are not bypassed; this email belongs to no group.
- **No unsubscribe link or List-Unsubscribe header.** `platform_notify` takes `accountSafety: true` instead of `unsubscribeUrl` (exactly one of the two; its own category `foundmill-account-safety`). The footer says why the email always arrives.
- **Demoted counts like removed.** The review names removal, but the same attack works with a demotion alone (a member cannot cancel a deletion either). A founder who left on their own does not get the cancel right.
- **`founder_departures`, written by a trigger.** A small business-scoped table filled by a security-definer trigger on `memberships`, so every path that lowers or deletes a founder's row is covered; its function now runs with `search_path = pg_catalog, public, pg_temp` and qualifies every table (P3-2). The person reads their own rows (they are no longer a member), a founder the project's; no client writes. Not exported (reason recorded).
- **One cancel link per email, in its own table** (P3-3, P3-4). `founder_cancel_links` holds one row per email that carries a cancel: the token's SHA-256 (256 random bits minted by the api, like the invitation link), the request it cancels and its end (that request's day). A newer email never replaces an older link. The row is deleted when the gateway answers that it did not send; when the gateway does not answer at all, the row is kept and the api logs it, since the email may have gone out (third review). The cancel names the request (from the link, or from the card) and is checked under the audit chain's lock: if another request has replaced it, the cancel is refused. Service role only, with its own RLS and cross-tenant test.
- **Names in Inbox notes** (P3-5): every person-written name in a note goes through `withoutLinks` and is markdown-escaped in the note's body, so a display name cannot become a link or markup there; the subject line, plain text, uses the unescaped names for a removal as for a role change (third review).
- **An admin's removal is announced** (P3-1): the recent demotion is read on the service connection before the removal's transaction (under the actor's RLS an admin could not read the project's departures). Read before, not inside, because on PGlite a second connection inside the transaction would wait for it; the tiny gap between the read and the removal does not matter, since the demotion row only grows older.
- **The removal's email carries the cancel too** when a deletion was already waiting (also a lowered role): the person is a former founder for that request whenever they left after it.
- **The Inbox has no per-person audience**, so the note is the project's: whoever reads the Inbox sees it, as they see roles in Settings › People.
- **"/home"** is not one route here: the signed-in front door is Today, the dashboards are Home, and someone with no project left lands on /new. The card is on all three (it renders nothing while there is no project to keep).
- **The base branch moves**: `team.ts` changes are kept to a few lines (the limit, the names and the demotion read before the transactions). At the merge of #120 its locked re-reads and the grant rule were taken as they are; the actor's limit on lowering a founder now sits after every access check (the editor's fresh row, the lock on the person's row, `assertWithinGrantorAccess`), so a change refused for access never spends one.

## Left out

- No eval: no agent behavior changed.
- No summary sent at the end of the window describing the state then: it needs a scheduled pass. One "More changes" email per window stands for the ordinary notices folded; essential ones are never folded into it, and past a person's own essential share one folded email for the day carries the latest cancel they may use (above).
- The founders' notices are not shown in-product beyond the Inbox notes and Settings › Preferences' latest emails ("Who runs a project, or its deletion"); a removed founder cannot read the stored copy of their email (the row is readable only by members).
- The Playwright spec needs the gateway recorder for `/a/<token>`; on the shared default stack (no `GATEWAY_URL`) that test skips and the card test still runs.
